Matched betting toolkit

Most offers are two steps: place a qualifying bet with your own money to trigger the bonus, then convert the resulting free bet into cash. Work through the sections in order for each offer.

1. Qualifying bet real bet, real stake

Use this for: the initial "risk-free" bet most offers require before they give you a bonus - e.g. "bet £20, get a £20 free bet." You're using your own money on both sides. Look for lay odds within about 1-2% of the back odds.

2. Free bet bonus money, not your own

Use this for: converting the free bet you were given (from the qualifying bet above) into cash. Most UK free bets are "stake not returned" - toggle below to match what your offer says. Aim for back odds 3.0-10.0 and 70%+ extraction.

3. Offer log track your progress

Use this for: logging the actual profit once an offer is fully settled (after both bets above have completed), so you can see progress toward your monthly target.
